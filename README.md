# FS22 – Food Safety & Post-Harvest Protection System

FS22 is an advanced non-chemical disinfection technology designed to reduce microbial contamination and post-harvest losses in food systems, particularly in grains and fresh produce.

## The Problem

Up to 25% of food production is lost globally due to post-harvest contamination, with fungi and mycotoxins being major contributors. This directly impacts:

- Food safety
- Public health
- Economic efficiency
- Environmental sustainability

Traditional chemical-based solutions present limitations, including environmental risks, regulatory constraints, and reduced effectiveness over time.

## The Solution

FS22 is a photonic-based disinfection system that operates without chemicals and can be integrated into industrial food processes such as:

- Grain intake
- Conveyor systems
- Storage environments
- Post-harvest processing lines

The system reduces fungi and microbial load while preserving food integrity and quality.

## How It Works

FS22 applies controlled photonic energy in a continuous flow system, allowing scalable treatment of food products in motion.

Key features:

- Continuous operation in industrial environments
- No chemical residues
- No impact on physicochemical food quality
- Compatible with existing infrastructure

## Results

Experimental results demonstrated:

- Up to 100% reduction in fungal incidence in grains (under specific conditions)
- Over 90% reduction in fungal colonies in fruits
- No negative impact on food quality or germination potential
